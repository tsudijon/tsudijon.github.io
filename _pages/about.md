# About Me.

I am a postdoctoral scholar at Wharton Statistics and Data Science, advised by [Edgar Dobriban](https://statistics.wharton.upenn.edu/profile/dobriban/) and [Eric Tchetgen-Tchetgen](https://statistics.wharton.upenn.edu/profile/ett/). 
Previously, I obtained my Ph.D. in Statistics at Stanford, where I worked with [Sourav Chatterjee](https://souravchatterjee.su.domains//) and [Lihua Lei](https://lihualei71.github.io/index.html). Before that, I worked in quant finance and studied Applied Math at Brown University.
